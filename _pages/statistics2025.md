The data below is taken from the integrated library system software and the OverDrive digital content platform, both used by all public libraries in Saskatchewan. These numbers highlight the growing patron base and widespread use of the public library system in Saskatchewan, the continued growth of both physical and electronic collections, and a degree of cooperation between public library systems that remains unprecedented in Canada.

Over the years, a number of changes to the data gathering and reporting methods, as well as changes among the libraries themselves, have caused some fluctuations in year-over-year numbers. Because of this, not all statistics are directly comparable. 

Influencing factors include:
  - Changes to the integrated library system software in 2009-10, 2013, and 2015. The current system has provided accurate and specific data since September 2015.
  - The Covid19 pandemic and resulting library shutdowns caused a large reduction in the use of physical collections in 2020 and to a lesser extent 2021, with a corresponding post-pandemic increase in the use of OverDrive digital collections. Physical circulation has largely rebounded, while use of OverDrive and library investment in digital formats continues to grow.
  - As of 2023, the counts of OverDrive E-Titles are more accurate than in previous years and counts of E-Items and Unique Borrowers of E-Titles have been added.
  - In 2018, Prince Albert Public Library, previously a branch of Wapiti Regional Library, became a separate municipal library. This change affected data for Prince Albert Public Library and Wapiti Regional Library.
  - In 2022, Lloydminster Public Library, previously not a part of the SILS consortium, joined Lakeland Library Region. In 2025, Lloydminster Public Library became a separate municipal library. These changes affected data for Lloydminster Public Library and Lakeland Regional Library.
